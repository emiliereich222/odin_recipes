Garlic Bread

Description

This recipe is amaing because of the delicious garlicky herb butter that the bread is smothered in. This bread carries a lot of flavor and will elevate any meal you bring it to. Everyone loves this recipe and you will too! This recipe is also customizable with the different herbs and cheeses you can use. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon dried parsley
  • 1 pound load Italian bread, cut into 1/2 inch slices
  • 8 ounces of shredded mozzarella cheese

Directions

  1. Gather all Ingredients
  2. Preheat the over to 350 degree F
  3. Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat; stir in garlic powder and dried parsely
  4. Place bread slices on a medium baking sheet. Using a basting brush, brush bread generously with melted butter mixture
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until lighly toasted, about 10 minutes
  6. Sprinkle bread with mozzarella and any remaining butter mixture. Continue baking until chees is melted and bread is lighly browned, about 5 minutes.
